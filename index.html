01 — Services

Pick the clean your car actually needs.

Every car arrives in a different state, so pick the level of care yours needs. Flat rates are listed below, no haggling at the driveway.

Interior detail

Vacuum throughout, upholstery and carpet cleaned, seats and trim wiped down, glass cleared, vents and cupholders worked out by hand.

Exterior detail

Hand wash, wheels and tyres cleaned properly, paint decontaminated, then a machine polish where the finish needs it and a protective wax to close.

Full detail

Interior and exterior together, in one visit. The option most people take when the car has not been touched in a while.

02 — How it works

Four steps, no guesswork.

  1. 1

    Tell us the car

    Call or book online. Year, make, model, and what you want sorted out. Photos help if the interior is rough.

  2. 2

    We quote it

    You get the price for your vehicle and the work it needs before we book anything. No surprises once we are on site.

  3. 3

    We come to you

    We arrive where the car is parked with our own equipment and work around your day. You do not need to drive anywhere.

  4. 4

    You look it over

    We walk the car with you before we leave. If something was missed, we sort it out then and there.

04 — Questions

Straight answers.

How do I get started?

Call 346-643-9249 or book online. Either way, tell us the vehicle, where it is parked, and what you want done.

Where do you work on the car?

Wherever it is parked, as long as we can get around it safely. A driveway, a kerbside spot, or a workplace car park all work.

Do you need my water or power?

Ask us when you call. It depends on the service and the site, so we would rather confirm it with you than promise it here.

What does it cost?

It depends on the vehicle, the service, and the condition it is in. We quote your car before we book it, so you know the price up front.

How long does it take?

An interior or exterior detail and a full detail are different jobs on different cars. We give you the time estimate with your quote.

Do you cover my part of Houston?

Call and tell us where you are. We will tell you straight away whether we can get to you.
